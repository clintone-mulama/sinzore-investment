# Sinzore Investment App - Design Guidelines

## Design Theme: Clean Minimalism

This application is strictly designed according to the **Clean Minimalism** design philosophy:
- **Palette**: Neutral cool tones, soft blues (`#0061A4`, `#D1E4FF`), crisp white backgrounds (`#FDFBFF`), and charcoal text/contrasts (`#1A1C1E`).
- **Typography**: Clean, standard sans-serif system fonts with generous letter spacing and consistent Material Design 3 type-scaling.
- **Card Styling**: High-contrast texts (using `onPrimaryContainer` / `onSurface`) to ensure perfect legibility and a flat, clean minimalist look.
- **Spacing**: Generous negative space, large paddings (e.g., 24dp for key elements), and standard 8dp/12dp spacing intervals.
- **Rules**: Do not introduce gradients, heavy drop shadows, or unstyled flat gray backgrounds. Keep every layout clear, bold, and modern.
